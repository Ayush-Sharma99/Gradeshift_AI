# KNOWN LIMITATIONS & ENGINEERING ROADMAP — GradeShift PrimePath

> **Transparency Principle:**  
> Industrial AI credibility depends on stating limitations as clearly as capabilities. This register documents every technical, statistical, and operational boundary of GradeShift PrimePath at checkpoint `v0.17.0-competition-ready`.

---

## 1. Current Technical & Validation Limitations

| # | Limitation | Root Cause in Current Checkpoint | Operational Mitigation in PrimePath | Path to Industrial Resolution (`E4`/`E5`) |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | **100% Synthetic Data (`E2`/`E3` Ceiling)** | All 18 transition episodes (`TRAIN`, `CALIBRATION`, `LOCKED_TEST`) are generated by the seeded CSTR/Erlang simulator (`src/gradeshift/simulate.py`). No HMEL historian tags, LIMS records, or ERP ledgers are present. | Every data object carries `Provenance.SIMULATED` or `Provenance.ASSUMPTION`, and every UI screen displays an explicit operating-mode banner. | Execute offline historical replay (`E4`) on 12–24 months of de-identified plant historian + LIMS transition logs via `ingestion.py`. |
| **L2** | **100% Abstention (`0` Natural Prime Candidates) on `LOCKED_TEST`** | Chronological whole-event splitting placed `A→B`, `B→A`, `A→C`, `C→A` in `TRAIN` (`10` events) and unseen directions `B→C`, `C→B` in `LOCKED_TEST` (`5` events, `235` rows). | **Intentional fail-safe behavior:** The train-only `ApplicabilityDetector` flags `B→C` and `C→B` as `UNSUPPORTED` (`ood` hard gate), forcing `ABSTAIN / FOLLOW SOP` and achieving `0.0 t` false-prime mass. *100% abstention is not commercial success; it is evidence that the current model refuses unsupported transitions.* | Expand training corpus so every commercially supported grade pair (`B→C`, `C→B`, etc.) has $\ge 5$ historical training episodes before enabling candidacy for that direction. |
| **L3** | **Empirical Conformal Under-Coverage on `LOCKED_TEST` (`0.6596` vs `0.90` Nominal)** | `SplitConformalCalibrator` is fitted on `141` decision rows drawn from only `3` calibration episodes (`EP-BC-00`, `EP-CA-01`, `EP-CA-02`). Within-episode residuals are temporally correlated, and `LOCKED_TEST` shifts to `B→C`/`C→B`. | Subgroup conditioning is automatically gated off when `< 5` episodes exist (`calibrator.py`), and the OOD applicability gate blocks prime candidacy on shifted directions. | Calibrate on $\ge 15$–$30$ independent historical episodes per grade direction using episode-level cluster/Mondrian conformal quantiles. |
| **L4** | **Single-Property Quality Scope (`MFI` Only)** | The POC models Melt Flow Index (`MFI`, g/10min, ASTM D1238) dynamically; polymer density (`g/cm³`), ash, volatiles, and gel count are carried as static grade context (`config.py`) or out of scope. | `PRIME_RELEASE_CANDIDATE` is strictly advisory and requires `Shift Quality Approver (QC)` sign-off under full plant QC protocol. | Add multi-property conformal joint intervals ($\text{MFI} \times \text{Density}$) where both online/inferred and LIMS measurements are available. |
| **L5** | **Simplified Residence-Time & Silo Routing Topology** | `material_identity.py` uses a calibrated CSTR + Erlang plug-flow transport delay (`25 min` transport lag, `150 min` mean residence time) on a single simulated line (`SIM-UNIT-1`). | Material eligibility gate (`material_service.py`) blocks prime candidacy whenever residence uncertainty exceeds `20.0 min`, route ambiguity exceeds `0.20`, or mapping quality is `AMBIGUOUS`/`UNAVAILABLE`. | Calibrate residence-time distribution (RTD) parameters per physical extruder/conveying line using tracer or step-change LIMS alignment data. |
| **L6** | **Parameterized Assumption-Based Economics (`E0`)** | Unit prices, downgrade spreads (`₹12,000–25,000/t`), false-prime penalties (`₹40,000–90,000/t`), and sample costs (`₹10,000–20,000`) are scenario assumptions in `config.ECON_SCENARIOS`. | Economic figures are strictly separated into `REALIZED VALUE`, `COUNTERFACTUAL VALUE`, and `ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIO` and never override policy gates. | Replace scenario constants with plant commercial margin tables approved by Supply Chain / Finance during shadow-mode pilot. |
| **L7** | **No Live OPC-UA / DCS / LIMS Integration** | This repository is a self-contained offline/replay and interactive workbench package without live plant network connectors. | Read-only architecture (`ingestion.py`, `RuntimeContext`) cleanly separates data adapters from domain evaluation. | Connect read-only historian (PI / OPC-UA) and LIMS query adapters in an isolated DMZ shadow-mode deployment (`E5`). |
