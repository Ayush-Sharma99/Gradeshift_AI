# DATA_CARD — GradeShift PrimePath Synthetic Corpus (`ds-v1`)

---

## 1. Dataset Identity

- **Dataset Version:** `ds-v1`
- **Feature Schema Version:** `feat-v1` (`37` causal features)
- **Generator Module:** [`src/gradeshift/simulate.py`](../src/gradeshift/simulate.py) & [`src/gradeshift/dataset.py`](../src/gradeshift/dataset.py)
- **Provenance:** **`SIMULATED`** (`E2` synthetic simulation). **Zero real HMEL plant or LIMS records are contained in this corpus.**
- **Random Seed:** `42`

---

## 2. Corpus Composition

- **Total Transition Episodes:** `18` (`3` episodes per grade-pair direction across `6` directions: `A->B`, `B->A`, `A->C`, `C->A`, `B->C`, `C->B`).
- **Grades (`src/gradeshift/config.py`):**
  - **Grade `A` (`HDPE-P`, HDPE Pipe PE100):** Target `MFI = 0.30 g/10min`, spec band `±20%` (`[0.24, 0.36]`), density `0.949 g/cm³`, dwell `30.0 min`.
  - **Grade `B` (`HDPE-BM`, HDPE Blow Moulding):** Target `MFI = 8.00 g/10min`, spec band `±5%` (`[7.60, 8.40]`), density `0.954 g/cm³`, dwell `30.0 min`.
  - **Grade `C` (`LLDPE-F`, LLDPE Film Grade):** Target `MFI = 1.00 g/10min`, spec band `±10%` (`[0.90, 1.10]`), density `0.918 g/cm³`, dwell `30.0 min`.
- **Simulated Signals per Episode:**
  - `H2_ratio`: Hydrogen-to-monomer gas ratio (`-`, sampled every `5 min`).
  - `bed_temp`: Reactor bed temperature (`degC`, sampled every `5 min`).
  - `MFI_online`: Lagged, noisy online analyzer reading (`g/10min`, sampled every `5 min`, analyzer lag `15 min`).
  - `lab_samples`: Periodic laboratory grab samples (`LabSample`) with explicit `collected_at` and delayed `result_at` (`~60 min` lab turnaround latency).
  - `routing`: Continuous `RoutingInterval` records (`DOWNGRADE` vs `PRIME`, production rate `~12 t/h`).

## 4. Target Definition & Causal Alignment

- **Prediction Target (`target_mfi`):** At decision time $t$, the target is the laboratory `MFI` of the sample whose `collected_at` matches the mapped downstream material window within the decision horizon (`240 min`), revealed only after `result_at > t`.
- **Causal Firewall:** Features at timestamp $t$ only access process observations with `timestamp <= t` and laboratory samples with `result_at <= t`. Pending samples (`collected_at <= t < result_at`) contribute only to `lab_pending_count`, never their unrevealed MFI value.

---

## 5. Known Dataset Limitations

1. **Purely Synthetic (`SIMULATED`):** Calibrated first-order CSTR / Erlang washout dynamics; does not capture real catalyst poison events, distributor fouling, or multi-modal molecular weight kinetics.
2. **Within-Event Correlation:** The `141` calibration rows come from only `3` independent transition events (`47` rows/event).
3. **Chronological Direction Shift:** Because events are generated by direction and split chronologically, `LOCKED_TEST` contains `B->C` and `C->B` transitions not seen in `TRAIN`.
